# Genres Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** People pick the genres they post about (once, then in Settings); the radar finds trends in 28 genres; Studio's Trending shows only topics in their genres, each card naming all its genres.

**Architecture:** The radar tags every item, static topic and emerging topic with genre ids and hands every genre to Supabase `radar_topics.genres`, while the Sheet and emails stay finance-only. A per-person `genres` settings row drives Studio's onboarding gate, its Trending filter and the Supabase engine's radar pick.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12 + pytest + SQLite (radar, engine); Postgres migrations (Supabase); vanilla JS + `node --test` (Studio).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-genres-design.md` (this repo).

## Global Constraints

- **Genre ids, in this order, with these names** (the contract between radar, Supabase, Studio and engine):
  Money & work: `personal_finance` Personal finance, `stock_market` Stock market & trading, `economy` Economy & policy, `wealth` Wealth & investing, `crypto` Crypto, `real_estate` Real estate, `business` Startups & business, `careers` Careers & jobs, `marketing` Marketing & creators.
  Tech & science: `technology` Tech & gadgets, `ai` AI, `science` Science & space.
  Sports: `cricket` Cricket, `football` Football, `sports` Other sports.
  Entertainment: `movies` Movies & Bollywood, `ott_tv` OTT & TV, `music` Music, `gaming` Gaming.
  Lifestyle: `health` Health & fitness, `food` Food, `travel` Travel, `fashion` Fashion & beauty, `auto` Cars & bikes, `spirituality` Spirituality & astrology.
  Society: `politics` Politics & current affairs, `education` Education & exams, `environment` Environment & weather.
- **Finance genres:** `personal_finance`, `stock_market`, `economy`, `wealth`, `crypto`. A topic with at least one is a finance topic.
- **Only finance topics** reach the Google Sheet's Radar topics tab, the HOT alert emails and the daily digest.
- `radar_topics.genres` is `text[]`, most relevant first, never empty. A person's genres are the `settings` row `genres`: comma-separated ids in the order above; missing or blank means "not picked yet".
- Radar `kind` values: `hot`, `capped`, `rising`, and new `latest` (the quiet-genre floor; Supabase only).
- $0: no AI, no paid APIs, no new keys or secrets.
- **Radar repo** (`F:\Truwealth`): work in a new worktree on branch `feat/genres`, cut from `origin/feat/radar-supabase` (PR #3). Commit per task. Tests: `F:\Truwealth\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q` from the worktree.
- **Ghostwriter repo** (`F:\Truwealth-Ghostwriter`, branch `feat/studio-web`): edit in place, **never commit or push** (the user commits). It holds other sessions' uncommitted work: before editing `web/index.html`, `web/data.js`, `web/dev-supabase.js` or `app/src/core/views.js`, check `list_sessions` for a running Truwealth session and `git diff --stat` for fresh edits, and change only the lines your task needs. Tests: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q`, `node --test "web/test/*.test.js"`, `node --test "app/test/*.test.js" "app/sim/*.test.js"`.
- **Order of going live:** apply migration `20261022120000_radar_genres.sql` before the radar genres PR merges (PostgREST rejects an insert naming an unknown `genres` column), before Studio's new code is used against the live project, and before the Supabase engine runs Task 9's code (it selects `genres`). Migrations are applied live only after the user says yes, through Claude in Chrome, never the connected Supabase MCP.

## Review Focus

1. A topic with finance and non-finance genres (home buying: `personal_finance`, `real_estate`) must still reach the Sheet; a `real_estate`-only topic must not (Task 4 test `test_mixed_genre_topic_still_reaches_the_sheet`).
2. Someone with no `genres` row, or a blank one, must get the genre screen in Studio and finance genres in the engine, never an empty or unfiltered feed (Task 7 `genres: nothing picked yet…`, Task 9 `test_snapshot_keeps_radar_topics_in_the_persons_genres`).
3. The radar database restored from the Actions cache has no `genres` columns; it must open, gain them and keep its rows (Task 2 `test_old_database_gains_genre_columns`).
4. A radar row inserted without genres (PR #3's code still running, or a row from before the migration) must get genres from its bucket, so Studio never hides it (Task 6 trigger + check).
5. A genre id spelled differently in one place (`ott` vs `ott_tv`) silently empties Trending for that genre (exact-list assertions in Task 1 and Task 7).
